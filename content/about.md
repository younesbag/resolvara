# About Resolvara

Resolvara is an independent, open project. It exists because AI support agents are increasingly billed per resolution, the vendor decides what counts as a resolution, and buyers have had no neutral way to check.

It is not owned by, sponsored by or affiliated with any support software vendor. It does not sell anything today.

## What it is

- **The Strict Resolution Standard.** An open, versioned definition of a resolution that a buyer can apply to their own data. The text is licensed CC BY 4.0.
- **The auditor.** A tool that applies the Standard to your export, in your browser, and gives you a dispute pack to take to your vendor. The code is licensed MIT.
- **The vendor index.** Each vendor's own published definition of a billed resolution, how to export the data, and what we could not confirm, with source links and a checked date.

## Principles

- Trust is the product. Every number is traceable to rows you can inspect, the definition is public and versioned, the code is open, and nothing is uploaded.
- Honest uncertainty. Repeat contact can mean a different issue, so it is shown as a flag with evidence, the window is adjustable, and recent conversations are held out until they can be judged.
- No vendor bashing. We show your data and the vendors' own published definitions with sources. Where a vendor fact could not be confirmed, we say so instead of guessing.
- Right of reply. If a vendor believes a statement about it is wrong, it can send a correction with a source, and we will fix it and note the change.
- Zero data custody. We cannot lose or leak the data we never receive.
